# Evidence Policy

Adapted from the original eight claim types and E5–H working labels. Keep three independent axes: learning importance, provenance, and evidence.

## Visible provenance

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| [Text] / [原文] | Material actually accessed; quote only exact verified words, label paraphrases as paraphrases |
| [Explanation] / [解释] | AI synthesis, interpretation or inference, with assumptions where consequential |
| [External] / [外部资料] | A separately identified source outside the book; identify actual access limits |
| [Unverified] / [待核实] | Insufficient basis; say what would allow verification |

A book's claim may be verified as its author's statement while its truth remains unverified. In that case use both provenance and verification status. Model recollection is not a verified external source. Labels are not certificates of correctness; use them near meaningful claims, not on every mundane sentence.

## Claim types

`source_statement` author wording/position; `empirical_finding` specific observations with design/sample/outcome; `scientific_consensus` scoped stable agreement with examined evidence; `theory` a model and assumptions; `interpretation` a reading of meaning; `hypothesis` a not-yet-established proposal; `personal_observation` the reader's actual report; `ai_inference` a conclusion added by AI. Split mixed claims. Faithful AI paraphrase can still be source_statement; added inference must be marked locally.

## Working evidence labels

| Level | Basis required |
|---|---|
| E5 | Mature, scoped consensus with substantial independent evidence and high-quality synthesis actually read |
| E4 | Multiple reasonably strong independent studies generally agreeing; explain design/directness/consistency |
| E3 | Some support with material disputes, limitations or conflicting results |
| E2 | Predominantly theoretical explanation with limited direct validation |
| E1 | Preliminary, exploratory or limited observations; a single study is not automatically poor quality |
| H | Hypothesis, AI inference or speculative personal explanation |
| not_assessed | No completed evidence assessment |
| not_applicable | Text attribution, definitions, literary interpretations or personal records where empirical grading is inappropriate |

These labels are inherited working conventions, not probabilities, clinical GRADE or proof of causality. Do not apply empirical grades mechanically to philosophical/literary arguments. State grading reasons and scope. Prestige, citation counts and a meta-analysis title do not justify E5. Check design, bias, overlapping samples, heterogeneity, replication, measurement and population relevance. Abstract-only/secondary access generally cannot establish E4/E5 independently: leave not_assessed or clearly provisional.

## Risk-proportionate checking

- Routine clarification: ground it in the accessible passage and correct obvious mistakes; no full audit on every sentence.
- Important definitions, quotations, statistics or central interpretations: inspect text/support and locator before reusable notes.
- Consequential causal claims, personal attribution, contested science, doubtful citations or public-use claims: use Claim Audit, check competing explanations and external research when available. If access is inadequate, withhold certainty and retain a question/hypothesis.

For each important claim separate “the author says X” from “X is true.” Read source material, not just search hits. Check whether the source both exists and supports this precise conclusion. A resolving DOI or correct ISBN proves neither. Never invent authors, papers, quotations, statistics, pages, edition mappings or verification dates.

Minimal source card: ID; known title/author/edition and actual address; locator; access date and scope (`full_text`, `section`, `excerpt`, `abstract`, `secondary`, `not_accessed`); supports/does_not_support. For studies add design, population, outcome, relevant uncertainty and limitations. Unknown details stay blank. Distinguish PDF file pages, printed pages and reader-dynamic pages; verify OCR quotes against page images when available.

For reflowable EPUB, displayed pagination can change with font, viewport and reader layout settings. Explain this limitation when asked to convert a reader page to a paper page; separate numbering alone does not explain why mapping is unavailable. A verified edition-matched page-list or fixed-layout mapping may support conversion, so do not claim all EPUB pages are inherently unmappable. Otherwise use the actual title/member/anchor or supplied passage.

Core reusable notes require faithful attribution, boundary-aware evidence and auditing of consequential claims. Hypotheses and uncertainty may be stored as such; blocked claims cannot circulate as established facts. Reader confirmation verifies their expression, never the world claim. Model self-review is not independent evidence.
